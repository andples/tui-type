# Themes from Monkeytype

The 25 themes below are adapted from [Monkeytype](https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype)'s
theme definitions (`frontend/src/ts/constants/themes.ts`) by the Monkeytype
contributors, licensed under the
[GNU General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype/blob/master/LICENSE).
These files keep that license; the rest of ttyp is MIT.

How they were adapted: ttyp's `bg`, `fg`, `sub`, `main` and `error` are
Monkeytype's `bg`, `text`, `sub`, `main` and `error`, and `correct` is `text`.
ttyp has no caret or `subAlt` colour, so those aren't used. `error_extra` is
not taken from Monkeytype: it's `error` blended 35% toward `bg`. Names are
lowercased with `-` for spaces.
